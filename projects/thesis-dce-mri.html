Multi-Scale Subtraction Consistency cGAN for Breast DCE-MRI Synthesis

  • Period: Master Thesis, KTH (published at ISMRM 2025 and MICCAI 2026)
  • Tech Stack: PyTorch, Deep Learning, GANs, Medical Imaging, Computer Vision
  • Thesis: www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:2051725/FULLTEXT01.pdf

Designed a contrast-free, multi-sequence conditional GAN (cGAN) that synthesizes multiphase breast DCE-MRI, removing the need for gadolinium contrast agents.

Proposed the Multi-Scale Subtraction Consistency (MSSC) loss, a novel generator-only objective that enforces clinically meaningful enhancement. The model outperformed Pix2PixHD baselines on both reconstruction and lesion-level metrics.

The work was published at ISMRM 2025 (Multiscale Subtraction Consistency-based GANs for Contrast-Agent-Free Breast DCE-MRI Synthesis) and extended into a MICCAI 2026 paper (Multi-parametric MRI for Contrast Agent Free Breast DCE-MRI Synthesis).
