Choir Voice Separation & Transcription

  • Period: Group project, KTH (Speech / Music Information Retrieval)
  • Tech Stack: Python, Onset Detection, Pitch Estimation, MIR Evaluation Metrics
  • Github: github.com/Khadorstorm/ChoirSeparation-Transcription

Designed and implemented the transcription pipeline for the Soprano and Bass voice parts, applying onset detection and pitch estimation algorithms to convert separated audio signals into symbolic note sequences.

Evaluated the transcriptions against ground-truth scores using standard MIR metrics (note precision/recall, F-measure); led the quantitative analysis and authored the results section of the final report.
