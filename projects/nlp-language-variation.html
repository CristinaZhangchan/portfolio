Language Variation over Time (NLP)

  • Period: Group project, KTH (Language Engineering)
  • Tech Stack: Python, NLTK, scikit-learn, PyTorch, NumPy, Jupyter Notebook
  • Project: drive.google.com/drive/folders/10i7y1Mg8V7Nv9rdGSlx_OFtFO3f1SEK3

Developed and refined models that classify documents by the decade in which they were written, using language engineering methods and TF-IDF document representations.

My focus was optimising accuracy across various algorithms (including multinomial logistic regression) on the historical dataset "Svenska partiprogram och valmanifest".
