Pawsitive Adoption

  • Period: December 2025 - March 2026
  • Tech Stack: React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Google Gemini API, Google Maps API, Docker, Supabase, Vercel
  • Github: github.com/CristinaZhangchan/Pawsitive-Adoption

Built a multilingual (EN/CN/SE) full-stack platform connecting users for pet adoption, hosting, dog-walking and vet consulting, with pet descriptions generated automatically by the Google Gemini API.

Integrated the Google Maps API for geolocation tracking, and handled Dockerised deployment and PostgreSQL schema design.
